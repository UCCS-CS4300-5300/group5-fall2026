How to Use Shelf Life

Getting Started

Shelf Life helps you organize the food in your pantry, fridge, freezer, and bag so you can keep track of where everything is stored and when it expires. The app also includes an AI Assisted Chef feature that can help suggest meal ideas based on the food you already have.

Adding Food

Open the Pantry Organizer from the Menu and search for the food item you want to add. After selecting the item, drag it into the Shelves, Fridge, Freezer, or Bag area to place it in your pantry. Some forms may also let you enter details like quantity and dates before saving.

Moving Food

You can move food items by dragging them from one storage area to another, such as from the Shelves to the Fridge or Freezer. You can also update an item’s location by opening its menu, choosing Edit, and changing the storage location there. Shelf Life will update the item’s stored location and expiration information when needed.

Viewing and Editing Items

Click the three-dot menu on any pantry item to open more options. From there, you can view item details such as category, quantity, storage location, expiration date, and status, or choose Edit to update the item.

Deleting Food

To remove an item, open the three-dot menu and choose Delete, or drag the item to the trash area if that option is available. This helps you remove items that were used, thrown away, or added by mistake.

Expiration Status

Each food item is labeled with a status such as Fresh, Expiring Soon, Expired, or Unknown. These labels help you quickly decide what should be used first and what may no longer be safe to keep.

AI Assisted Chef

The AI Assisted Chef uses the food currently in your pantry to suggest recipe ideas and meal inspiration. This can help you make better use of the ingredients you already have and reduce food waste.
